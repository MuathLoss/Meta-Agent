# UI Quality Rubric

Use this rubric to judge whether a frontend screen looks premium or AI-made.

Score each category from 1 to 10.

## 1. Layout structure

10 = screen has clear sections, strong alignment, balanced density, and obvious flow  
5 = usable but sections feel generic or loosely arranged  
1 = random cards/elements with weak structure

## 2. Visual hierarchy

10 = title, subtitle, primary action, secondary actions, and content priority are obvious  
5 = hierarchy exists but many elements compete  
1 = everything has similar weight

## 3. Spacing and alignment

10 = spacing feels intentional and consistent  
5 = some inconsistent gaps or awkward padding  
1 = cramped, floating, or messy

## 4. Typography

10 = strong title scale, readable body, useful muted text, consistent sizing  
5 = readable but plain  
1 = inconsistent or hard to scan

## 5. Component polish

10 = buttons, cards, badges, tables, and forms feel like one design system  
5 = components work but feel mixed  
1 = components look patched together

## 6. Color and contrast

10 = dark maroon/charcoal theme feels premium and readable  
5 = acceptable but generic  
1 = random colors, weak contrast, or overdone gradients

## 7. SaaS maturity

10 = feels like real enterprise/product software  
5 = decent student project look  
1 = obvious AI-generated dashboard

## 8. Responsiveness

10 = desktop/tablet/mobile are clean  
5 = mostly okay with some awkward wrapping  
1 = breaks or overflows

## 9. Functional safety

10 = no behavior/API/auth/tenant logic changed  
5 = minor risk or unclear preservation  
1 = risky changes

## 10. Overall polish

10 = ready for demo/client screenshots  
5 = acceptable but not impressive  
1 = still looks like AI slop

## Score interpretation

90-100 = premium  
75-89 = good, needs polish  
60-74 = acceptable but still generic  
Below 60 = needs redesign pass
